About CoastalOS

Built by hotel owners, for hotel owners.

CoastalOS is a technology-enabled hotel management and operating platform for independent and boutique hotels. It brings hospitality, technology, operations and asset management together, so owners can grow revenue, run leaner and see clearly how their property performs.

Our origin

Developed inside real hotels, not a software lab.

CoastalOS grew out of years of owning and operating independent hotels. The platform was shaped by the daily work of running properties: filling rooms, looking after guests, managing teams and answering to owners.

That is why it centralizes the work that can be done once, well, and leaves the hospitality itself to the people on site.

What we believe

A simple philosophy, applied property by property.

  • Centralize what can be centralized.Reservations, pricing, marketing and back-office work run once, for every property.
  • Automate what can be automated.Routine steps happen in the background, so staff time goes to guests.
  • Keep hospitality human.On-site teams focus on clean rooms, a warm welcome, and solving problems in person.

Who we work with

Independent properties with their own character.

CoastalOS works with owners and operators who know their properties well and want the systems and support of a larger operation, while keeping their independence, their brand and their guests.

  • Independent hotels
  • Boutique hotels
  • Motels
  • Inns
  • Resorts
  • Typically around 15 to 100 rooms.

Our focus

Four outcomes guide everything we do.

Grow revenue

Revenue management, distribution, marketing and centralized rate control, with a steady push toward direct bookings.

Reduce operating costs

Technology, centralization and automation, with efficient staffing and clear oversight of payroll and labor.

Modernize the guest experience

Easy booking, fast communication, convenient check-in, and support that responds when guests need it.

Give owners visibility

Reporting on revenue, occupancy, rates, expenses, labor and property performance, all in one place.
