About CoastalOS

Built by hotel owners, for hotel owners.

CoastalOS is a technology-enabled hotel management and operating platform for independent and boutique hotels. It takes on the work every hotel repeats, such as pricing, reservations, payroll and reporting, and runs it centrally, so owners keep their independence with the support of a larger operation.

Our origin

Developed inside real hotels, not a software lab.

CoastalOS grew out of years of owning and operating independent hotels. The platform was shaped by the daily work of running properties: filling rooms, looking after guests, managing teams and answering to owners.

That experience shapes how the platform is built: the work that repeats at every property is run centrally, and the hospitality itself stays with the people on site. See how the platform works.

Who we work with

Independent properties with their own character.

CoastalOS works with owners and operators who know their properties well and want the systems and support of a larger operation, while keeping their independence, their brand and their guests.

  • Independent hotels
  • Boutique hotels
  • Motels
  • Inns
  • Resorts
  • Typically around 15 to 100 rooms.

Our approach

Why an operator-built platform.

Independent owners usually choose between buying software or handing the hotel to a management company. CoastalOS takes a different route.
