About CoastalOS

Traditional Hospitality Values. Next-Generation Performance.

CoastalOS is a technology-forward third-party hotel management company built for independent hotels. We run the hotel day to day while the owner keeps ownership of the property.

About us

Your hotel has decades of history and incredible untapped potential. But today’s travelers book differently, and rooms price dynamically by the minute. We take full-service management off your shoulders, implementing the latest marketing and revenue technology to capture the revenue your property is currently leaving on the table.

CoastalOS was built by the team behind Coastal Hospitality Group, which has acquired, developed and operated independent hotels since 2012. See our services

Technology Is Our Engine. Hospitality Is Our Soul

Preserving Identity

Enhancing Experience

Building Value
