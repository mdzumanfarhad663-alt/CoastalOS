About CoastalOS

Traditional Hospitality Values. Next-Generation Performance.

CoastalOS is a technology-forward third-party hotel management company built for independent hotels. We run the hotel day to day while you keep ownership of your property.

Untapped potential, modern performance

Your hotel has decades of history and incredible untapped potential. But today’s travelers book differently, and rooms price dynamically by the minute. We take full-service management off your shoulders, implementing the latest marketing and revenue technology to capture the revenue your property is currently leaving on the table.

“We built CoastalOS from firsthand experience operating independent hotels. Our mission is to help owner-operators maximize their property’s potential while transitioning them from running the business to simply owning it.”

Jed Tarr founded Coastal Hospitality Group in 2012 with the acquisition of a small beach motel in San Clemente, California, which he personally operated. Since then, he has grown the company into a portfolio of 14 independent hotels, motels and RV resorts across the country.

By centralizing management and back-office operations across its portfolio, Coastal creates economies of scale that reduce operating costs, improve performance and increase asset value.

Today, through CoastalOS, that same operating platform is available to third-party hotel owners—giving their properties the benefits of professional management, modern technology and AI-driven operations while allowing them to retain ownership of the asset they worked to build.
