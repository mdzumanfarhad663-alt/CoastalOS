The operating model

Run once. Delivered to every property.

Every hotel repeats the same work: setting rates, answering booking questions, running payroll, closing the books, reporting to the owner. When each property does all of it alone, the effort and the cost are repeated too.

CoastalOS handles that shared work once, centrally, with one team and one set of tools, and delivers it to each property. Your on-site team keeps the parts of the job that only they can do.

Each function runs on its own tools and people, and the owner is left connecting the pieces.

The principles

Three rules behind every decision.

  • Centralize what can be centralized.Pricing, reservations and the books are run by one experienced team for every property, rather than rebuilt at each hotel.
  • Automate what can be automated.Confirmations, reminders and routine reports go out on their own, so nobody has to remember to send them.
  • Keep hospitality human.When a guest needs something in person, the people at your property have the time to give it.

Questions owners ask

About the platform.

Is CoastalOS software I install?

No. CoastalOS is an operating platform: our team, technology and processes working together to run the core functions of your hotel, centrally.

What size of property is it built for?

Independent and boutique hotels, motels, inns and resorts, typically around 15 to 100 rooms.

Do we keep our own brand?

Yes. Your property keeps its name, website and guests. CoastalOS works behind the scenes to grow direct business under your brand.

What happens in a property review?

We look at how your hotel runs today and where centralization and technology could add revenue, lower cost, or improve visibility.
