Revenue & Distribution

Pricing, marketing and channels managed together, with a steady focus on bookings that come to you direct.

  • Revenue managementData-driven pricing and inventory decisions across every channel your property sells on.
  • Marketing & distributionSearch, social, email and channel management that keeps your property visible and brings guests back.
  • Direct bookingReservations through your own branded website, so more guests book with you directly.
02Modernize the guest experience

Reservations & Guest Support

A central team that looks after guests before, during and after their stay.

  • Central reservationsCalls, emails and booking requests handled centrally, in line with your property’s policies.
  • Guest communicationClear, timely messages before arrival and during the stay, so guests know what to expect.
  • Guest supportResponsive help when guests need it, with your on-site team kept informed.
03Reduce operating costs

Property Operations

Your on-site team stays focused on guests, with the structure behind them handled centrally.

  • On-site team managementSupport and oversight for the people who run your property day to day.
  • Hiring, scheduling & trainingHelp finding the right people, planning schedules and bringing new team members up to speed.
  • Operational controlsConsistent standards and checks that keep the property running the way it should.
04Reduce operating costs

Technology & Automation

Modern hotel technology set up and run for you, so routine work happens in the background.

  • Property management technologyThe core systems a hotel runs on, set up and managed as part of the platform.

Clear numbers and back-office support, so owners always know how the property is performing.
