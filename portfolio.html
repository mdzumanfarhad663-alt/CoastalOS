Case study Sample

A 40-room coastal motel moves to a central operating model.

The challenge

Pricing, reservations, payroll and reporting were each handled separately, and the owner pieced together performance from four systems.

What changed

Revenue management, reservations and owner reporting moved to the central team. The on-site team kept guest service, housekeeping and maintenance.

The outcome

One monthly owner report, faster responses to guests, and clearer control of labor and rates in the first year.
