Questions owners ask

Before you get in touch.

Is CoastalOS software I install?

No. CoastalOS is an operating platform: our team, technology and processes working together to run the core functions of your hotel, centrally.

What size of property is it built for?

Independent and boutique hotels, motels, inns and resorts that want to keep their own brand and guests.

Do we keep our own brand?

Yes. Your property keeps its name, website and guests. CoastalOS works behind the scenes to grow direct business under your brand.

What happens in a property review?

We look at how your hotel runs today and where centralization and technology could add revenue, lower cost, or improve visibility.
