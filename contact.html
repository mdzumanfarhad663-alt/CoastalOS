Before you get in touch

Is CoastalOS a management company or software?

CoastalOS is a third-party hotel management company. We take responsibility for the day-to-day operation of your hotel, using modern hotel technology, automation and AI-driven revenue management to run it.

What does CoastalOS manage day to day?

Revenue management, marketing, reservations, staffing, guest service, accounting and administration. The on-site hotel team, managed by CoastalOS, looks after housekeeping, maintenance, property care and physical guest needs.

Do I keep ownership of my hotel?

Yes. You own the property and retain the asset. You receive reporting and financial results and set high-level ownership objectives. We run the hotel.

Can you help me step back from daily operations?

Yes. CoastalOS takes responsibility for day-to-day operations, employees, reservations, guest service and administration. Let’s discuss a seamless transition plan.

Will my hotel keep its name and character?

Yes. We don’t turn independent properties into sterile, automated chains. Your property keeps its name, and we work to grow direct business under it.

How does technology support personal guest service?

We use technology to eliminate administrative bottlenecks, allowing property staff to focus on face-to-face guest care.

What is the complimentary assessment?

A complimentary AI-driven revenue and operational assessment of your hotel, to help you discover your property’s untapped value. Request a call to get started.

How does a management partnership begin?

Request a call. We start with discovery and a review of your property, then plan the transition before CoastalOS takes over day-to-day management.

What size of property is it built for?

Independent, non-brand-affiliated hotels, motels, inns, resorts and boutique properties.

What do we discuss on the call?

How your property runs today, what you’d like to improve, and whether a management partnership with CoastalOS is the right fit.
