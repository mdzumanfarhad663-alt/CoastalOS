Before you get in touch.

Is CoastalOS software I install?

No. CoastalOS is an operating platform: our team, technology and processes working together to run the core functions of your hotel, centrally.

What size of property is it built for?

Independent and boutique hotels, motels, inns and resorts that want to keep their own brand and guests.

Do we keep our own brand?

Yes. Your property keeps its name, website and guests. CoastalOS works behind the scenes to grow direct business under your brand.

What happens in a property review?

We look at how your hotel runs today and where centralization and technology could add revenue, lower cost, or improve visibility.

How long does onboarding take? Sample

Most properties are fully on the platform within 30 to 60 days of signing, depending on the systems in place and the services chosen.

Does CoastalOS work with our existing systems? Sample

We review your property management system, channel manager and accounting tools during the property review, keep what works and plan any changes with you.

What does an agreement look like? Sample

Scope, fees and term are set out in a written agreement after the property review, so you know exactly what is covered before anything starts.

Can we choose individual services? Sample

Yes. Some owners start with selected service groups, such as revenue management or owner reporting, and add more over time.
