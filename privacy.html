Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by CoastalOS, built by the team behind Coastal Hospitality Group, 711 S El Camino Real, San Clemente, CA 92672. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to CoastalOS.

Information we collect

When you request a property review or a call, we collect the details you choose to give us:

  • Your name and work email address
  • Your property’s name, location and number of rooms
  • Whether you would like a review or a call, and any message you add

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and arrange a property review or call
  • To prepare and discuss the review with you
  • To keep the website secure and working properly

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run this website and handle requests, such as our website host and email or form-processing services. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers. Links to property websites open sites that have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up on your request, and then delete them unless we need to keep them for legal or business-record reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have given us. If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what we collect and to request deletion. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

To make a request, email info@coastalhospitalitygroup.com.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you send us. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is intended for hotel owners and operators. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@coastalhospitalitygroup.com or (877) 350-0053.
