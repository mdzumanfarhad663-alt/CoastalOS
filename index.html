The opportunity

Your hotel shouldn’t require an entire company to operate it.

Every independent property carries the same jobs: reservations, front desk, pricing, guest messages, marketing, payroll, bookkeeping and reporting. Usually each one is handled separately, property by property.

Modern technology makes it possible to run much of that work once, centrally, and well. That leaves your on-site team free for the hospitality only they can deliver.

Each function runs on its own tools and people, and the owner is left connecting the pieces.

The CoastalOS platform

One operating platform. Every critical function.

CoastalOS centralizes the work behind a great hotel, using modern technology to drive efficiency and grow direct business.

How CoastalOS operates.

A simple philosophy, applied property by property.

  • Centralize what can be centralized.Reservations, pricing, marketing and back-office work run once, for every property.
  • Automate what can be automated.Routine steps happen in the background, so staff time goes to guests.
  • Keep hospitality human.On-site teams focus on clean rooms, a warm welcome, and solving problems in person.

Questions owners ask

Before you get in touch.

Is CoastalOS software I install?

No. CoastalOS is an operating platform: our team, technology and processes working together to run the core functions of your hotel, centrally.

What size of property is it built for?

Independent and boutique hotels, motels, inns and resorts, typically around 15 to 100 rooms.

Do we keep our own brand?

Yes. Your property keeps its name, website and guests. CoastalOS works behind the scenes to grow direct business under your brand.

What happens in a property review?

We look at how your hotel runs today and where centralization and technology could add revenue, lower cost, or improve visibility.
