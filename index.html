What is CoastalOS

CoastalOS is an operating platform for independent and boutique hotels. It brings together a central hospitality team, modern technology and proven operating systems to run the core functions of a hotel: revenue, reservations, marketing, staffing support and reporting. Owners get the advantages of a larger hotel company while keeping ownership of their property, their brand and their guests, with regular reporting so they always know how the property is performing.
